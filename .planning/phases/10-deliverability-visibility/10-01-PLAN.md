<objective>
DLVR-04 — make a failed send VISIBLE and durable on the admin view instead of a
silent drop, without leaking participant emails anywhere a participant can see.

Today an invitation row is inserted only on a SUCCESSFUL send
(send-invites.ts onConflictDoNothing on result.ok); a failed or rate-limited send
persists nothing, so the failure lives only in the transient form response and
vanishes when the organizer navigates away. This plan (a) persists a per-recipient
delivery_status (additive/nullable, migration 0008), (b) records it for every
attempted recipient across BOTH senders via upsert, (c) surfaces it on the
admin-only whos-responded card reusing the existing chip meta, and (d) locks the
access boundary with a canary so no participant surface can ever read it.

SC1/SC2 (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment + real-inbox human verification) are NOT in this
plan — they are Dave's human checks handled by 10-02. This plan is the
agent-closable half: SC3, SC4, SC5.

Output: migration 0008 applied locally; delivery_status recorded + surfaced
admin-only; canary + full suite green.
</objective>
